What the model does

Pace uses Tobler's hiking function, slowed by load and thin air above 1,500 m. Energy uses the Pandolf load-carriage equation with the Santee downhill correction. Pack weight falls day by day as food is eaten. A day is flagged when it runs past your target hours, burns over 4,000 kcal, or puts most of its work after the halfway point.
